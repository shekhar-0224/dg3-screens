04Balance Card

FieldFormatDefinition
Balance$84,210 largeCash only. Deposits minus deployed capital minus reserved capital, plus realized P&L from settled positions. Does not include unrealized P&L; an open position's paper gain is not spendable until it is closed or settles.
Today delta+$2,140 today (+2.6%)Cash balance change since local midnight (trader's device timezone), not a fixed UTC cutoff. Reflects settled P&L and deposit/withdrawal activity, not unrealized movement. Resets and recomputes at local midnight, not at a single global moment.
+ DepositGold primary buttonOpens deposit flow.
WithdrawGhost buttonOpens withdrawal flow.
Deployed$31,640 (38%)Total cost basis of all open positions. Percentage is of total balance.
Reserved$5,730 (7%) goldCash currently locked in open BUY limit orders, not yet filled. Released back to Balance if the order is cancelled or modified to a smaller amount. SELL limit orders reserve shares, not cash, so they do not contribute here.
Unrealized+$1,872 (+5.9%)Mark-to-market gain/loss on open positions, shown for context. Percentage is on deployed capital. Informational only; not part of Balance.
Why Balance excludes unrealized P&L
Balance is meant to answer "what can I actually deploy right now." Mixing in unrealized gains would overstate spendable capital, since that value isn't liquid until a position closes or the market settles. Unrealized P&L stays visible as its own line and its own KPI tile, just not folded into the headline number.

CLV is always expressed in cents (¢), never as a percentage. 1¢ = 0.01 probability. A +3.2¢ average means entries were 3.2 probability points better than closing line on average.

09Equity Curve

ElementFormat / Behavior
SVGviewBox="0 0 580 160", 210px height, preserveAspectRatio="none". Data spans x=20 to x=560 (20px padding each side). Line, fill, trade dots, cursor, snap dot all in one SVG.
Period tabs7d / 30d / 90d / All. Default 30d. Each tab calls eqSetPeriod() which redraws line+fill paths, repositions end dot, updates date labels, pct, and re-renders trade dots via eqRenderDots().
Trade dotsColored circles placed directly ON the line at data vertex coordinates. Position is computed from EQ_TRADE_PTS[period] which maps each trade to a ptIdx in the current period's pts array. vx = 20 + ptIdx * (540/(n-1)), cy = pts[ptIdx].y. Dots are always on the line -- no hardcoded x positions. Green = WON, red = LOST.
Same-date groupingMultiple trades at the same pt index are spread vertically: eqDotOffsets(n) returns evenly spaced y offsets centered on the data point (e.g. 2 trades: -3px and +3px). A thin connector line links them. Hover on any dot in the group shows all trades stacked.
Hover on dotShows trade card only: market name · WON/LOST badge · side · P&L · CLV. No price data shown on dot hover.
Hover on lineDashed vertical cursor + snap dot on line. Tooltip shows: date · $ balance · % change from period start. No trade section unless cursor is on a dot.
Date labels5–7 short-form labels per period. Container has padding:4px 3.5% 10px to align with SVG 20px data padding. Updated on period switch via appendChild.
Width60% of analytics row (flex:3). Risk metrics 40% (flex:2).

Editable fields

FieldContent
Limit priceNumeric input, prefilled with current limit. Label shows original value ("was 0.48") for reference.
SharesNumeric input. Unfilled order: prefilled with the full order shares, label "Shares" + "was [N]". Partial fill: prefilled with only the remaining unfilled shares (not the original order size), label changes to "Shares (unfilled portion)" + "was [remaining N]". Filled shares already settled are untouched either way.

Balance impact preview

ElementContent
New total valueLive recalculation: new limit price x new shares. Updates as the trader types.
Balance impact (BUY)Compares new total against the amount currently reserved. Unfilled order: baseline is the full original total. Partial fill: baseline is only the value of the remaining unfilled shares, since that is the only amount still reserved. If new total > baseline: "Additional balance reserved" in red, showing the extra amount that gets locked. If new total < baseline: "Balance released" in green, showing the amount returned to available balance. If unchanged: "$0".
Balance impact (SELL)"No balance impact" - sell orders reserve shares, not cash, so modifying price or size does not touch the cash balance.
NoteUnfilled: "Modifying replaces the existing order. Balance reserved for the original order is released and the new amount is reserved instead." Partial fill: "Filled shares ([N]) are unaffected. Only the unfilled portion of this order is modified."

Actions

ElementContent
Save changesGold primary button. Cancels the existing order and places a new one at the updated price/shares. Closes modal, shows "Order updated" toast with market name.
DiscardGhost button. Closes modal, no action.
Mechanics
A modify is a cancel-and-replace under the hood, same as Polymarket's own order book. The trader never sees "cancel" language here since from their side it is one edit, but balance impact must reflect the full delta between old and new reserved amount, not just the new order in isolation.

17Share Cards

Three card types. All share the same modal shell. Cards are designed for screenshot + Share on X (primary CTA).

Meta line
The context line below the badge shows league/event only. "Polymarket" and "Open position" are stripped from the displayed string. Example: "NBA Playoffs · Game 4 · Resolves Apr 28, 2026" not "NBA Playoffs · Polymarket · Open position".
Modal behavior
Share card modals render as a fixed overlay above the current page with a semi-transparent backdrop (rgba 0,0,0,0.45). Clicking the backdrop or pressing Escape closes the modal. Both trade share and profile share modals are placed outside the main app container in the DOM so position:fixed renders correctly at all times.

21Metrics & Calculations

Defines how every portfolio-level number is derived, and which order/position states feed into each one. Reference for engineering; no UI changes implied by this table alone.

MetricCalculationIncluded / excluded
BalanceDeposits - deployed - reserved + realized P&LCash only. Excludes unrealized P&L; an open position's paper gain is not spendable until it is closed or settles. This is the number used to determine what a trader can actually place new orders with.
ReservedSum of (limit price x shares) across open BUY limit orders, unfilled portion onlyDeducted from Balance the moment a BUY order is placed, not on fill. Released back to Balance if the order is cancelled or modified to a smaller amount. Never included in P&L; it is a reservation, not a trade. SELL limit orders reserve shares, not cash, so they are excluded from this figure entirely.
DeployedSum of cost basis (avg entry x shares) across open positions onlyExcludes cash reserved for open orders. Open orders are pending, not deployed.
Unrealized P&L(current market price - avg entry) x shares, summed across open positionsOpen orders excluded entirely; they have no entry price yet, so no unrealized P&L exists for them. Shown as its own line/KPI, never folded into Balance.
Realized P&L(exit price - avg entry) x shares, summed across settled/closed positionsOnly counted once a position is closed or a market settles. An order being placed, modified, or cancelled never generates P&L.
Win rateSettled positions with P&L > 0 divided by total settled positionsOpen positions and open orders excluded. Only finished trades count.
Volume tradedSum of cost basis across all trades, open + settledCounted once an order fills and becomes a position. An unfilled or cancelled order contributes $0 volume.
CLVClosing line price - entry price, in cents, per outcomeOnly meaningful for pre-match entries; in-play entries excluded from the average since CLV mechanically mirrors win/loss there. Open orders excluded; CLV needs a confirmed entry price.
Core rule
An open order can change Balance and Reserved (BUY orders lock cash) but never changes Deployed, Unrealized P&L, Realized P&L, Win rate, or Volume. Those five only react to filled, open, or settled positions. Modifying or cancelling an order only ever moves the reservation between Balance and Reserved; it never touches P&L. Unrealized P&L is always informational and never added into Balance, even though both are shown on the same card.
